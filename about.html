About Peak Landscapes LLC

Locally Owned. Veteran Owned. Built for Colorado Living.

Our Story

Rooted in Colorado

Peak Landscapes is a veteran owned and operated landscape boutique specializing in full designs and installations. We pride our selves on professionalism, integrity, and customer satisfaction. It is our pleasure to create an outdoor oasis customizable to each of our clients needs. We understand that communication is a vital aspect during the landscaping project process, and we strive to maintain clear and consistent communication throughout every project. Our founder, a proud Marine veteran, started Peak Landscapes to bring top-notch landscaping and snow removal services to Colorado, combining military precision with a love for the outdoors.

Based in Woodland Park, we serve the Colorado Front Range and surrounding counties with landscaping in the warm months and snow removal all winter.

  • Colorado-specific design and materials
  • Honest estimates & clear timelines
  • Clean job sites and professional execution
  • Residential & commercial services
